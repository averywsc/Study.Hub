Biology

Achievement Standard: AS91153 (Micro-organisms)

Key Notes

Micro-organisms Summary

  • Micro-organisms include bacteria, viruses, fungi, and protozoa, each with distinct structures and life cycles.
  • Bacteria are prokaryotic, reproducing by binary fission, and can be beneficial or pathogenic.
  • Viruses require a host cell to replicate and are not considered fully living organisms.
  • Understand the conditions micro-organisms need to grow: nutrients, temperature, moisture, and pH.
  • Be able to explain how the body's immune system responds to pathogenic micro-organisms.
