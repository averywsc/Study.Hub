Achievement Standard: AS91240 (Extreme Natural Event)

Key Notes

Extreme Natural Event Summary

  • An extreme natural event is a natural hazard that has significant impacts on people and the environment.
  • Understand the physical processes that cause the event (e.g. plate tectonics for earthquakes).
  • Distinguish between primary effects (direct results) and secondary effects (knock-on consequences).
  • Compare short-term and long-term responses, including relief efforts and rebuilding strategies.
  • Use a specific case study with real place names, dates, and statistics to support your answers.
