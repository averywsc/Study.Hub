About Student Hub

A study platform built for Year 12 and 13 students preparing for NCEA externals.

Resource Library

Key notes, past exam papers, and grade exemplars for common NCEA subjects, organised by standard so you can find exactly what you need.

Group Finder

Browse or post study groups by subject, level, and mode, plus a built-in study timer and discussion prompts to keep sessions focused.

Why It Exists

Study resources are usually scattered across the internet with no NCEA-specific structure, and finding other students to revise with is mostly luck. Student Hub puts both in one place, built specifically for the lead-up to externals.

Privacy

The site doesn't collect personal data or require an account to browse. Group listings only show subject, level, time, and how to join, keeping things anonymous and simple.
