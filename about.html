About Student Hub

What this site is, and who it's built for.

Student Hub is a study platform built for Year 12 and 13 students preparing for NCEA externals. It brings together two things students usually have to find separately: a resource library and a way to connect with other students.

The Resource Library covers key notes, past exam papers, and grade exemplars for common NCEA subjects, organised by standard so you can find exactly what you need.

The Group Finder lets you browse or post study groups by subject, level, and mode (online or in person), along with a built-in study timer and discussion prompts to help sessions stay focused and productive.

The site doesn't collect any personal data or require an account to browse, keeping it simple, private, and easy to use during exam season.
