History

Achievement Standard: AS91247 (Historical Event)

Key Notes

Historical Event Summary

  • Identify the causes of the event, distinguishing between long-term, short-term, and trigger causes.
  • Explain the perspectives of different groups involved, and how their views may have differed.
  • Discuss the consequences of the event, both immediate and longer term.
  • Use specific evidence, including dates, names, and key facts, to support your explanations.
  • Practice structuring essay paragraphs with a clear argument, supporting evidence, and analysis.
