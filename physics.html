Achievement Standard: AS91168 (Electricity and Electromagnetism)

Key Notes

Electricity and Electromagnetism Summary

  • Understand Ohm's Law (V = IR) and how it applies to series and parallel circuits.
  • Calculate power (P = VI) and energy in electrical circuits.
  • Explain how a magnetic field is produced around a current-carrying wire.
  • Describe how electromagnetic induction generates a current, and its real-world applications.
  • Practice circuit diagrams and always include correct units in your final answers.
